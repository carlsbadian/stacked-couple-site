If your sleep is off or your stress is high, nothing else in your stack works as
well. Sleep, focus, and stress aren't soft variables — they show up in the same
numbers as your training and your protocol. This is where you fix the
foundation.

## What you'll get here

- **Better sleep, measured** — what's worth trying, what actually moved our sleep data, and what didn't.
- **Focus that lasts the workday** — the habits and tools that earn a place, tested against real days.
- **Stress you can manage** — how it shows up in your results, and what to do about it.
- **Doing it with a partner** — why two people holding the same standard makes it easier to keep.
- **The honest midlife conversation** — aging parents, what decline actually looks like, and what's possible instead.

## Why it comes first

A stressed, under-slept system doesn't respond well to anything — training,
nutrition, or peptides. Get this stack right and every other one gets easier.
