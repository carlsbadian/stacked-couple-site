Nothing replaces the training. Supplements, peptides, and tracking can amplify
it — they can't do it for you. This stack is about a routine you can actually
keep with a full schedule.

## What you'll get here

- **Strength training that respects recovery** — programming built for where your body is now, not where it was at 25.
- **Short, hard conditioning** — HIIT that fits a busy week.
- **Mobility and recovery** — what rest days actually look like, and the work that keeps you training.
- **Outdoor training** — rucking and getting outside, the Southern California way.
- **Home and hotel workouts** — for when there's no full gym, or you're on the road.
- **Hot yoga, honestly reviewed** — what changed, what it cost, and whether it earned its slot.
- **Real workout logs** — weight, form, and progress over time, plateaus included.

## The approach

Consistency over intensity. Batch what you can, log what you can measure, and
expect plateaus alongside the progress. A skipped session isn't a failure —
it's data.
