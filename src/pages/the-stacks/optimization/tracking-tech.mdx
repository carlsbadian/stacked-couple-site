Without measurement, you're guessing. This is the hardware and software we
actually run — what we measure, what we ignore, and how we read the numbers
without letting them run the show.

## What to measure

- **Wearables** — sleep stages, HRV, resting heart rate, and recovery trends (Oura ring, Apple Watch).
- **Smart scales and body scans** — weight is one data point; body composition is the story.
- **Tracking apps** — where your logs live, and how to get them talking to each other.

## What you'll get here

- Device reviews after months of real use, not unboxings.
- How to set a baseline, and what counts as signal versus noise.
- The weekly check-in: which numbers we look at, and which we deliberately don't.
- What to do when the tracker and your body disagree.
