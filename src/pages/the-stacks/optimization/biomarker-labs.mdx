Wearables read the outside. Labs read the inside. Without a baseline, there's no
way to know whether anything you're doing is working.

## The approach

- **Baseline before anything.** Know your starting point before you change a thing.
- **Retest on a schedule.** Verify changes instead of assuming them.
- **Let the data drive changes.** Adjust based on your panel, not on how one week felt.

## What you'll get here

- Which panels we run, how often, and what each one is actually for.
- How to prep for a blood draw so results compare cleanly over time.
- Reading your own results — reference ranges versus optimal ranges, in plain language.
- Retest stories: what moved, what didn't, and what we changed because of it.
