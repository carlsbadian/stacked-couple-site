Targeted means exactly that: something you add for a specific, tracked outcome,
on top of your daily foundation — which lives in [Fuel](/the-stacks/fuel/). The
goal isn't a longer list. It's knowing what each thing is doing for you.

## The test every add-on has to pass

1. **A named goal.** Sleep depth, recovery, focus, joint support — something specific.
2. **A number to move.** If you can't point to the marker it should shift, it doesn't go in.
3. **A review date.** Everything gets re-justified. No permanent passengers.

## What you'll get here

- What we take for which goal, and why.
- What the tracking showed after a fair trial — including the ones that did nothing.
- What we cut, and why. Knowing what to skip saves as much as knowing what to take.
