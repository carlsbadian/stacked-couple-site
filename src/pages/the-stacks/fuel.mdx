You don't need a perfect diet. You need a framework you can follow most of the
time — one that supports your training and the rest of your stack.

## What you'll get here

- **Protein and hydration targets** — the non-negotiables, and the macro targets that anchor everything else.
- **Quick meals from real food** — practical, not a cooking class.
- **One framework, two different goals** — how his plate and her plate differ while following the same playbook.
- **Fasting windows, explained** — what intermittent fasting is and isn't. Educational, not prescriptive.
- **Paleo and Whole30, minus the dogma** — the useful parts, explained simply.
- **Your daily foundation** — the baseline nutrition and supplementation everything else builds on.
- **Eating well on the road** — keeping the framework when you're away from your own kitchen.

## Where the line is

Your daily baseline lives here. Goal-specific supplements — the things you add
for a specific, tracked outcome — live under
[Optimization → Targeted Supplements](/the-stacks/optimization/targeted-supplements/).
Get the foundation right first; it isn't the experiment.
